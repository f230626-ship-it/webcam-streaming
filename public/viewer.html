Security Indicators Observed During Testing

OS-level (macOS): When the capture client activates the camera, a green dot appears in the menu bar (top-right). When the microphone is active, an orange dot appears next to it. These are OS-level indicators that appear regardless of which browser or application accesses the camera/mic.

Browser-level: Safari/Chrome show a camera icon in the tab or address bar when the site has active camera access. A green dot appears in the tab itself.

Hardware-level: The green LED next to the camera lens on the MacBook illuminates when the camera sensor is powered on. This is hardwired to the camera power supply and cannot be disabled via software.

Conclusion: All three indicator levels (OS, browser, hardware) were active during testing on macOS with Safari.
